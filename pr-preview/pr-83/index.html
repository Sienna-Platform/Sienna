Why Choose Sienna

Open solutions for a transforming industry

  • Cutting-edge modeling capabilities

    Scalable modeling capabilities powered by Julia with the rapid evolution of open-source development.

  • Extensibility

    Open codebase and permissive licensing (BSD 3.0) enable endless customization, reuse, and commercialization opportunities.

  • Efficiency by design

    Designed with efficiency in mind, Sienna promotes efficient, reproducible workflows that allow users to identify issues early and generate results quickly.

about NLR

Focused on today's Energy Challenges

From breakthroughs in fundamental science to new clean technologies to integrated energy systems that power our lives, researchers at the National Laboratory of the Rockies are transforming the way the nation and the world use energy.
